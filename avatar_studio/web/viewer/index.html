How to photograph

Add as many as you have. The model decides which photo is the front, the back, or a side. Full body, feet together, camera at chest height. Each photo adds a minute or two.

  • Front T-pose — arms straight out. Sets shoulders, chest, waist, hips, and thigh length.
  • Left side — arms down and slightly forward, not out to the side. Sets thickness and bust. Add the right side the same way if you can.
  • Back T-pose — same arms-out pose. Checks shoulders and hips.
  • Three-quarter — turn about 45°. Sets the bust.
