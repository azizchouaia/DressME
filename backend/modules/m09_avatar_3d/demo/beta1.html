How it works

Pieces: phone photo → background removed (pretrained BiRefNet) → texture cleaned of creases → mapped on a CC0 3D garment of that type, at your size, fit and fabric weight.

3D: each piece is pre-built for your body (MPFB + Blender, cloth simulation for big sizes) when you save it, so wearing it is instant.

Outfits: M2 scores your pieces with the pretrained OutfitTransformer on FashionCLIP vectors, the weather, the occasion and your wear log (M3: laundry and recently worn pieces are left out).
